Questions people ask

Can't find yours? Ask the assistant.

How do I request an appointment?

Tell the assistant what you would like done, add a photo if it helps, and give your name, mobile number and the days that suit you. The salon confirms by text or phone.

Is my appointment booked straight away?

No. The assistant offers times as requests. Nothing is booked until the salon confirms it with you.

How much will it cost?

Every price on this site is a from price. Colour and long or thick hair can cost more, so the stylist confirms the final price at your appointment.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, for a first colour or tint, at least 48 hours before the appointment. The salon arranges it when you request your time.

Can I send photos?

Yes. Use the camera button in the chat to attach up to two photos at a time, such as your hair now and the style or shade you like. The stylist sees them in your request.

What if I have allergies or a sensitive scalp?

Tell the assistant and it will pass that on to your stylist, who will talk it through at your appointment. The assistant cannot give advice on skin or allergies.

I had a reaction after a treatment.

If you have swelling of the face or lips, trouble breathing, spreading hives or severe burning, call 999 or go to A&E now. For anything milder, speak to a pharmacist, your GP or NHS 111, and tell the salon.

Do you do hair extensions?

No. The salon doesn't offer hair extensions, perming, straight-razor shaves or medical treatments.

Am I talking to a real person?

The chat is an AI assistant. It collects your details and passes them to the salon, whose team are real people. It can make mistakes, so the salon checks everything before confirming your appointment.

What happens to what I type and send?

Your messages and photos go to Claude, an AI service, to write the replies. In this demo, the details and photos from a finished request are kept for 30 days so the salon can see them, and then deleted. A real site would have its own privacy notice.
