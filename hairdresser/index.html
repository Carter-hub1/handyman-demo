How the assistant answers

It sticks to what the salon offers, quotes only the listed prices and never gives advice on skin or health. Examples of its replies:

How much is a balayage?

From £110. The stylist confirms the final price at your appointment, because it depends on your hair's length and thickness.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, for a first colour or tint, at least 48 hours before. The salon can arrange it when you request your appointment.

Can I get extensions put in?

Sorry, the salon doesn't do extensions. I can help with a cut, colour or an event style if you'd like.

Can I come on a Monday?

The salon is closed on Mondays. I can ask for Tuesday 10am to 12pm, Thursday 2pm to 5pm or Saturday 9am to 12pm.

How a request works

No waiting on hold and no form to fill in.

  1. Chat to the assistant

    Say what you'd like and add a photo of your hair or the look you want. It asks a few short questions, one or two at a time.

  2. The salon gets a summary

    Your name, mobile number, service, hair history and photos land on the salon's phone in one tidy list.

  3. The salon confirms

    They confirm your time by text or phone on a working day. Nothing is booked until they do.
