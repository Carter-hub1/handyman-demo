How an enquiry works

No waiting on hold and no form to fill in.

  1. Tell the assistant

    Say what needs doing and add a photo if you like. It asks a few short questions and checks the job is something Fraser covers.

  2. Fraser gets a job sheet

    Your name, address, the job, your photos and when suits you land on Fraser's phone in one tidy list.

  3. Fraser confirms

    He confirms a visit and the price by phone, text or WhatsApp on a working day. Nothing is booked until he does.
