Services

Small jobs for homes. Every job starts with a free quote.

Furniture assembly

Wardrobes, beds, desks and shelving built, and fixed to the wall where needed.

Useful to know when you ask: What the items are, how many, and which rooms they go in.

TV, shelf and mirror mounting

TVs, floating shelves, mirrors and curtain poles fixed securely to the wall.

Useful to know when you ask: What is being mounted, how big or heavy it is, and what the wall is made of.

Doors, hinges and locks

Sticking doors, loose hinges, new handles and replacement locks.

Useful to know when you ask: Which door, what it does, and whether it is a front door or an inside door.

Taps, toilets and sealant

Dripping taps, running toilets, and worn sealant round baths and showers.

Useful to know when you ask: Which room, what is happening, and whether you can turn the water off.

Painting and touch-ups

Patch, sand and repaint walls, trims and ceilings.

Useful to know when you ask: Which rooms, roughly how big, and whether anything needs preparing first.

Fences, gates and decking

Rotten fence panels, broken gate latches and loose deck boards replaced.

Useful to know when you ask: What is damaged, roughly how much, and how to get into the garden.

Wall and ceiling repairs

Holes, cracks and dents filled and made good, ready for painting.

Useful to know when you ask: How many, roughly how big, and which rooms.

Small odd jobs

A list of little jobs that never get done? Fraser does them together in one visit.

Useful to know when you ask: A rough list, and which rooms they are in.

What Fraser doesn't do

Electrical work, gas work, roofing, tree removal, asbestos and structural work need a registered specialist. Tell the assistant anyway and it will point you in the right direction.

Prices depend on the job, so Fraser gives a quote after he has seen the details or your photos.
