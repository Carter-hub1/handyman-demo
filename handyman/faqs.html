Questions people ask

Can't find yours? Ask the assistant.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the job, so Fraser gives a quote once he has the details. Quotes are free. He will either visit or ask for photos first.

Can I send photos?

Yes. Use the camera button in the chat to attach up to two photos at a time. Fraser sees them on your job sheet. Photos help him work out what tools and parts to bring.

I have a leak. What should I do?

If you can do it safely, turn off the water at the stopcock and keep away from electrics if water is near them. If anyone is at risk, call 999. Fraser doesn't do out-of-hours call-outs, so if you need someone tonight, look for a 24-hour emergency tradesperson.

I can smell gas. What should I do?

Call 999 and the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999. Don't use switches or naked flames, and open windows if it is safe. Fraser doesn't do gas work.

Do you do electrical or gas work?

No. Fraser doesn't do electrical work, gas work, roofing, tree removal, asbestos or structural work. For those you need a registered specialist.

Do you work at weekends?

Saturday mornings, 9am to 1pm. Fraser is closed on Sundays.

Do you cover my area?

Fraser works in Livingston, Broxburn, Bathgate, Whitburn, Linlithgow and nearby. If you are somewhere else, give the assistant your postcode and Fraser will confirm.

I rent my home. Can you help?

Yes, with your landlord's permission for anything that changes the property. Landlords can also ask about jobs across several properties.

Am I talking to a real person?

The chat is an AI assistant. It collects your details and passes them to Fraser, who is a real person. It can make mistakes, so Fraser checks everything before he confirms a visit.

What happens to what I type and send?

Your messages and photos go to Claude, an AI service, to write the replies. In this demo, the details and photos from a finished enquiry are kept for 30 days so Fraser can see them, and then deleted. A real site would have its own privacy notice.
