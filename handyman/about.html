About Fraser

A local handyman, not a call centre.

Fraser's Handyman does small jobs for homes in West Lothian. Fraser reads every enquiry himself and confirms every visit himself.

The assistant on this site takes your details when Fraser is on a job, so enquiries are not missed. It can't diagnose problems, give prices or promise a visit. Those are Fraser's decisions.

How the assistant helps

It asks what needs doing, checks it is not an emergency, looks at any photos you send, and collects your name, address and phone number. Fraser gets a job sheet with all of it in one place, ready to act on.

For a real business, this space holds: years trading, insurance details, any trade scheme memberships and a photo of the owner.
