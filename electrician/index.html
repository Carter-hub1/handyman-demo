How an enquiry works

No waiting on hold and no form to fill in.

  1. Tell the assistant

    Say what is wrong or what you want done. It asks a few short questions and checks the job is something Sam covers.

  2. Sam gets a job sheet

    Your name, address, the job, how urgent it is and when suits you land on Sam's phone in one tidy list.

  3. Sam confirms

    He confirms a visit by phone, text or WhatsApp on a working day. Nothing is booked until he does.
