Questions people ask

Can't find yours? Ask the assistant.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the property and the job, so Sam gives a quote once he has the details. Quotes are free. He will either visit or ask for photos first.

Is it safe to keep using it if my breaker keeps tripping?

Leave the breaker off, don't keep resetting it, and don't open the consumer unit. Tell the assistant, and Sam will mark it as urgent.

What if I smell burning or see sparks?

If there is smoke or fire, or someone has had a shock, call 999 straight away. If there is a hot, scorched or buzzing socket or fuse box, switch the main switch off only if the area is dry and safe to reach, keep people away and tell the assistant. Sam doesn't do out-of-hours call-outs, so if you need someone tonight, look for a 24-hour emergency electrician.

The whole street has no power. Can you help?

Call 105 (free) to report a power cut. That is for the network, not for an electrician. If only your own property has lost power, tell the assistant.

Do you work at weekends?

No. Sam works Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm.

Do you cover my area?

Sam works in Livingston, Broxburn, Bathgate, Whitburn, Linlithgow and nearby. If you are somewhere else, give the assistant your postcode and Sam will confirm.

Will I get the right paperwork for the job?

It depends on the job. Sam will tell you which paperwork applies when he quotes.

Am I talking to a real person?

The chat is an AI assistant. It collects your details and passes them to Sam, who is a real person. It can make mistakes, so Sam checks everything before he confirms a visit.

What happens to what I type?

Your messages go to Claude, an AI service, to write the replies, and this demo doesn't keep them. A real site would have its own privacy notice.
