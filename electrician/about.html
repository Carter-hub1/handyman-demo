About Sam

A local electrician, not a call centre.

Sam's Electrical works for homes and small businesses in West Lothian. Sam reads every enquiry himself and confirms every visit himself.

The assistant on this site takes your details when Sam is on a job, so enquiries are not missed. It can't diagnose faults, give prices or promise a visit. Those are Sam's decisions.

How the assistant helps

It asks what is happening, checks the problem is not an emergency, and collects your name, address and phone number. Sam gets a job sheet with all of it in one place, ready to act on.

For a real business, this space holds: years trading, qualifications, the electrical scheme the business is registered with, insurance details and a photo of the owner.
