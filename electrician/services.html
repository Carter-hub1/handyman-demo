Services

Electrical work for homes and small businesses. Every job starts with a free quote.

Fuse box upgrades

Old fuse box, or a consumer unit with no RCD protection? Sam swaps it for a modern one.

Useful to know when you ask: Roughly how old the fuse box is, and whether you are selling or renting out the property.

Rewires

Full and part rewires for houses and flats, planned around the rest of your building work.

Useful to know when you ask: Property size, whether it is lived in, and any plans for new rooms.

Sockets and lighting

Extra sockets, new lighting, downlights, outside lights, cooker and shower circuits.

Useful to know when you ask: Which rooms, and roughly how many points you want.

EV charger installation

A home charger fitted on a dedicated circuit, with the work tested before you use it.

Useful to know when you ask: Your car and charger make if you have chosen one, and where you park compared with the fuse box.

Inspection and testing

Electrical condition checks for landlords, buyers and anyone who wants to know where they stand.

Useful to know when you ask: Property type, number of floors, and whether you need the report for a sale or a tenancy.

Fault finding

Tripping breakers, dead sockets, flickering lights and circuits that have stopped working.

Useful to know when you ask: What stopped working, when it started, and whether it trips straight away or after a while.

Alarms, cameras and outdoor lighting

Smoke and heat alarms, security cameras and garden or driveway lighting.

Useful to know when you ask: Which parts of the property you want covered.

Extensions and garden offices

Power, lighting and consumer unit work for new builds and outbuildings.

Useful to know when you ask: Drawings or a rough sketch, and the planned start date.

Please don't open the fuse box

If something is tripping or has stopped working, leave the consumer unit cover closed and don't keep resetting a breaker that trips. Tell the assistant what happened and Sam takes it from there.

Prices depend on the property and the job, so Sam gives a quote after he has the details.
